## Privacy

- Synthetic test (90 sentences): injected values removed PERSON 24/30, PHONE_NUMBER 30/30, EMAIL_ADDRESS 30/30 (overall **93.3%**).
- Real meetings: the speech recognizer heard a participant name in 12 captures; stored captures still containing one of the 15 names: **2**.
- Raw audio is deleted from Redis right after transcription; the database holds only redacted text; only redacted text is sent to the LLM.
